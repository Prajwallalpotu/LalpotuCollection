A little more clarity

Before you choose.

How do I place an order?

Tap “Order on WhatsApp” or a collection card. Share the style you like and confirm the current price, availability, payment method and delivery details with the shop before ordering.

Do you deliver outside Nanded?

Our official Instagram profile advertises all-India delivery. Share your PIN code on WhatsApp to confirm delivery availability, charges and estimated timing for your order.

Can I ask for a specific colour or budget?

Yes—send your preferred colour, occasion, category and budget in your message. Ask to see currently available options before making your selection.

What are the payment and exchange terms?

Please confirm accepted payment methods and the exchange or return terms directly with the shop before paying. These details are not published here because we have not verified a current policy.
